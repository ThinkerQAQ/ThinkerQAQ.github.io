Architecture patterns name recurring structures and their trade-offs. They are not mutually exclusive: a modular monolith can be layered and event-driven internally; services can use DDD boundaries and read/write projections.

Choose from required qualities and organizational constraints: consistency, latency, scale, release independence, failure isolation, team ownership, operational maturity, and cost.

The best pattern is the simplest one that satisfies the real constraints with acceptable evolution risk.
