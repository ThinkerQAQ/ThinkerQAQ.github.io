Resilience begins with **deadlines/timeouts** so failures do not hold resources indefinitely. Retries should be bounded, backoff/jittered, inside an overall deadline, and used only when the operation's idempotency/unknown-outcome semantics make them safe.

Bulkheads/capacity limits isolate failures; circuit breakers avoid repeatedly calling a clearly unhealthy dependency; load shedding rejects work that cannot meet service objectives.

Fallbacks must be semantically valid and observable. A fallback returning plausible but wrong data can be worse than a clear failure.
