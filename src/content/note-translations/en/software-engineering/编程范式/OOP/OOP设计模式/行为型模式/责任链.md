Chain of Responsibility passes a request through an ordered set of handlers. A handler may process, transform, reject, short-circuit, or delegate to the next element.

Middleware/interceptor pipelines are common examples. Keep handler order explicit and define error/short-circuit semantics; otherwise cross-cutting behavior becomes difficult to debug.
