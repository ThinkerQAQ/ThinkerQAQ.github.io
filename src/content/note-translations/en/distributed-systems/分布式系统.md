## 1. What Is a Distributed System?

A distributed system is composed of multiple independent processes or nodes that communicate over a network and cooperate to provide a service.

## 2. Why Do We Need Distributed Systems?

A single machine has finite compute, storage, and I/O capacity and is also a single failure domain. Distributing a system across multiple machines can improve scalability, throughput, availability, and fault tolerance, although it introduces coordination and consistency problems.
