Operating-system storage management covers address spaces, virtual-memory mapping, physical-page allocation, caching, reclaim, and interfaces to persistent storage.

Virtual memory is an **address-space abstraction**, not “RAM plus disk.” Pages may be anonymous, file-backed, shared, resident, reclaimed, or swapped depending on mappings and pressure.

The OS tries to balance locality, protection, utilization, and I/O cost while presenting processes with simpler memory and file abstractions.
