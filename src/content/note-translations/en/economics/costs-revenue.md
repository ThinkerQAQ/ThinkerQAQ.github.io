Fixed costs do not vary with current output over the relevant horizon; variable costs do. Average cost divides total cost by output, while marginal cost is the incremental cost of another unit.

Economic cost includes opportunity cost, not only accounting cash expenses. Profit maximization is a marginal decision: expand output while marginal revenue exceeds marginal cost, subject to the market structure and operational constraints.
