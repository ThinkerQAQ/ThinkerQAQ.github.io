## 1. Accounting Cost and Economic Cost

Business decisions should consider not only cash expenses but also the value of alternative uses of resources.

- **Explicit costs**: actual monetary payments such as wages, rent, and materials.
- **Implicit costs**: the best forgone return from using owned resources in the current activity.
- **Economic cost**: explicit costs plus implicit costs.

Implicit cost is best understood as part of opportunity cost, not simply as a subjective feeling of loss.

## 2. Short-Run Cost

In the short run at least one input is fixed, so:

`TC = FC + VC`

where:

- `FC`: fixed cost;
- `VC`: variable cost;
- `TC`: total cost.

Related measures include:

- `AFC = FC / Q`;
- `AVC = VC / Q`;
- `ATC = TC / Q`;
- `MC = ΔTC / ΔQ`.

## 3. Marginal Cost

Marginal cost is the additional cost of producing one more unit of output.

In the standard short-run model, diminishing marginal product eventually causes marginal cost to rise. `MC` crosses `AVC` and `ATC` from below at their minimum points because a marginal value pulls an average upward when it is above the average and downward when it is below.

## 4. Long-Run Cost

In the long run all inputs can be adjusted. The long-run average cost curve summarizes the minimum unit cost available at each output level after the firm can choose its scale.

Falling long-run average cost can reflect economies of scale from:

- specialization;
- more efficient equipment;
- spreading fixed costs;
- purchasing or organizational advantages.

At sufficiently large scale, coordination and organizational complexity may create diseconomies of scale.

## 5. Revenue

If price is `P` and output is `Q`:

- total revenue `TR = P × Q`;
- average revenue `AR = TR / Q`;
- marginal revenue `MR = ΔTR / ΔQ`.

Only a price-taking firm has `P = AR = MR`. A firm with market power usually faces a downward-sloping demand curve, making marginal revenue lower than price.

## 6. Profit

Economic profit is:

`π = TR - Economic Cost`

The general marginal condition for profit maximization is:

`MR = MC`

The firm must still verify that the point is actually profit-maximizing and whether production should continue. For a perfectly competitive firm, the short-run shutdown decision is commonly related to price versus average variable cost.

## 7. Accounting Profit vs. Economic Profit

A business can report positive accounting profit while earning roughly zero economic profit once the opportunity costs of owners' capital, time, and other resources are included.

Zero economic profit therefore does not mean that the business earns nothing. It means its return is roughly sufficient to cover all explicit and implicit opportunity costs.
