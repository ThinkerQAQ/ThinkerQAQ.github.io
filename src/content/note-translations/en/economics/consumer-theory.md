Consumer theory models choice under a budget constraint. Preferences rank bundles; utility is a convenient representation of those rankings rather than a directly measurable physical quantity.

An interior optimum balances the marginal rate of substitution against relative prices, subject to the budget. Price changes create substitution and income effects. Real consumers also face information limits, behavioral biases, and transaction/friction costs.
