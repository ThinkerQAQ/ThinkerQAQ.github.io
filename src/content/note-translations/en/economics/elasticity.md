Elasticity measures percentage responsiveness. Price elasticity of demand compares the percentage change in quantity demanded with the percentage change in price; related measures include supply elasticity, income elasticity, and cross-price elasticity.

Elasticity depends on substitution options, necessity, market definition, and time horizon. It also affects revenue: with elastic demand, a price increase can reduce total revenue; with inelastic demand, the same change may increase it, all else equal.
