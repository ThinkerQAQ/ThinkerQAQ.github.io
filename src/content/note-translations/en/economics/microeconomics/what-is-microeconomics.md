## 1. Object of Study in Microeconomics
- **Price** is at the core
    - Price is a measure of resource scarcity
- The object of study is an individual economic unit
    - **Consumers** (residents, households)
    - **Producers** (firms, enterprises)
- It studies the economic behavior of individual units
    - Consumers: **purchasing behavior aimed at maximizing satisfaction**
    - Producers: **production behavior aimed at maximizing profit**
- Ultimately, these points mean that microeconomics studies resource-allocation problems
    - What to produce
    - How much to produce
    - For whom to produce
