## 1. Explicit Costs and Implicit Costs
### 1.1. Explicit Costs
- Expenditures actually incurred during production are explicit costs.
- Also called accounting costs.
### 1.2. Implicit Costs
- Losses perceived by the operator during production are implicit costs.
- Also called opportunity costs.
### 1.3. Economic Cost
- Economic cost = accounting cost + opportunity cost.
    - ![](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/TDoct/images/master/1608445540_20201220142344200_13340.png)

## 2. Short-Run Costs
### 2.1. Cost Function
- Reflects the relationship between cost and output.
- ![](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/TDoct/images/master/1608446491_20201220143758024_10536.png)
    - This cost is the minimum cost and output is the maximum output.
- Short-run total cost = short-run fixed cost + short-run variable cost = spending on fixed factors + spending on variable factors.
    - STC = TFC + TVC

### 2.2. Analysis
- Table method
![](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/TDoct/images/master/1608446493_20201220144005978_2885.png)
    - TFC: total fixed cost
    - TVC: total variable cost
    - TC: total cost
    - AFC: average fixed cost
    - AVC: average variable cost
    - AC: average cost
    - MC: marginal cost

## 3. Long-Run Costs
### 3.1. Long-Run Total Cost Analysis
- LTC and STC have similar curve shapes.
- Scale and cost
    - ![](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/TDoct/images/master/1608448958_20201220152135389_24506.png)
    - Each point on the long-run cost curve is the minimum cost among different scales.
    - All short-run cost curves lie above this long-run cost curve.

### 3.2. Long-Run Average Cost
- Both LAC and SAC are U-shaped.
- ![](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/TDoct/images/master/1608449577_20201220152557174_9530.png)
    - Long-run average cost is the envelope of short-run average cost curves.
    - Long-run cost is the minimum of short-run costs.
- Economies of scale means long-run average cost falls as output expands; it is related to, but not the same concept as, returns to scale.
    - ![](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/TDoct/images/master/1608449579_20201220153243089_7796.png)

### 3.3. Long-Run Marginal Cost
- LMC is also U-shaped and passes through the minimum point of LAC.
- At the output level determined by SAC and LAC, SMC = LMC.
- ![](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/TDoct/images/master/1608449581_20201220153252987_3721.png)

## 4. Revenue and Profit
- Total revenue = price * output.
- Average revenue = total revenue / output = price.
- Marginal revenue = change in total revenue / change in output.
- ![](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/TDoct/images/master/1608449933_20201220153729503_16431.png)
    - The sum of marginal revenues equals total revenue.
