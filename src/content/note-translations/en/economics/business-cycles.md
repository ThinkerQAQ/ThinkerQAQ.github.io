Business cycles are fluctuations in aggregate economic activity around longer-run trends. Expansions typically involve rising output and employment; contractions involve weakening activity, though indicators do not move perfectly together.

Cycles can reflect demand shocks, supply shocks, financial stress, policy changes, expectations, and global events. There is no fixed cycle length, and identifying turning points in real time is difficult.
