## 1. Classification of Unemployment
### 1.1. Natural Unemployment
#### 1.1.1. Frictional Unemployment
Unemployment that occurs while people leave one job and spend time searching for another.
#### 1.1.2. Structural Unemployment
Unemployment caused by changes in market competition or production technology, often because some industries expand while others contract. It can last longer than frictional unemployment because workers may need retraining or relocation.
