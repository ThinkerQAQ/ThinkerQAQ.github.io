## 1. History of Macroeconomics
Classical economics: a highly simplified classroom summary — emphasizes price adjustment, market clearing, and supply-side mechanisms.
Keynesianism: a highly simplified classroom summary — emphasizes aggregate demand, price/wage stickiness, and the stabilizing role of policy.
Neoclassical economics: a highly simplified classroom summary — emphasizes optimizing behavior, expectations, and market mechanisms.
New Keynesian economics: a highly simplified classroom summary — studies price/wage stickiness and policy effects with microeconomic foundations.

## 2. What Is Macroeconomics?
Compared with microeconomics, macroeconomics studies the **laws of economic operation** using **aggregate statistical data**.
