GDP measures the market value of final goods and services produced within an economy over a period. Real GDP adjusts for price changes; GDP per capita is a rough measure of average material output/income, not a complete measure of welfare.

Long-run growth depends heavily on productivity, capital, human capital, technology, institutions, and labor-force dynamics. Compare growth in real/per-capita terms when the question concerns living standards.
