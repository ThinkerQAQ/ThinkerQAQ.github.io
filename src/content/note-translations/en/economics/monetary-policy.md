Monetary policy influences financial conditions through policy rates, balance-sheet operations, liquidity facilities, communication, and expectations. Transmission works through borrowing costs, asset prices, exchange rates, credit, and spending decisions.

Policy acts with lags and under uncertainty. Lower rates do not mechanically produce a fixed amount of growth or inflation; the effect depends on expectations, banking/financial conditions, supply capacity, fiscal policy, and external shocks.
