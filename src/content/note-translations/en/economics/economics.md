## 1. What Is Economics?
Economics is the science of how people make **choices** under **limited resources**.

## 2. Basic Assumptions in Economics
- The economic-man assumption
    - Economic man means a rational person
        - Producers: pursue profit maximization
        - Consumers: pursue satisfaction maximization
- The complete-information assumption
