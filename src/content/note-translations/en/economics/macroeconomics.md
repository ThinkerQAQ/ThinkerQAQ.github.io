Macroeconomics studies aggregate production/income, employment, inflation, interest rates, financial conditions, and long-run growth. Short-run fluctuations and long-run productive capacity involve different mechanisms and policy trade-offs.

Aggregate identities such as GDP accounting describe relationships but do not by themselves establish causality. Expectations, institutions, global linkages, financial conditions, and policy credibility all matter.
