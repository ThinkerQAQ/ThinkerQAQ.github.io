Fiscal policy changes government spending, transfers, and taxation. It can affect aggregate demand in the short run and productive capacity/incentives in the long run.

The size of a fiscal multiplier depends on economic slack, monetary response, openness, financing, timing, household/firm behavior, and policy composition. Debt sustainability depends on future primary balances, interest rates, growth, currency/institutional conditions, and credibility—not on one universal debt threshold.
