Producer theory studies how firms transform inputs into output and choose input/output levels under technology and market constraints. Marginal product measures the additional output from another unit of an input, holding relevant factors fixed.

Short-run diminishing marginal returns are distinct from long-run returns to scale. Profit maximization compares marginal benefit/revenue with marginal cost, but actual decisions also depend on capacity, uncertainty, financing, regulation, and strategic interaction.
