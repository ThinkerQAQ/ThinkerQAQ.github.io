Inflation is a sustained rise in the general price level, not simply an increase in one price. Unemployment measures people without work who meet the relevant labor-force definition and are seeking/available for work.

The inflation–unemployment relationship is not a permanent mechanical trade-off. Expectations, supply shocks, productivity, labor-market institutions, and monetary/fiscal conditions change the short-run relationship.
