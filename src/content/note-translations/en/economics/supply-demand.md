A demand curve describes how much buyers are willing to purchase at different prices, while a supply curve describes how much sellers are willing to offer. Their intersection gives a market-clearing equilibrium in the simplified competitive model.

A movement **along** a curve comes from a price change; a shift comes from another determinant such as income, technology, expectations, input costs, or the number of participants. Price ceilings/floors can create shortages or surpluses when binding.
