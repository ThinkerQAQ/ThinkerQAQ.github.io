Market structure depends on the number and size of firms, product differentiation, entry barriers, information, and strategic interaction. Perfect competition is a benchmark with price-taking firms; monopoly has a single dominant seller; oligopoly emphasizes strategic interdependence.

Real markets often sit between textbook categories. Market power should be evaluated from substitution possibilities and barriers rather than from company count alone.
