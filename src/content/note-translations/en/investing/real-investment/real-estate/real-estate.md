## 1. Overall Home-Buying Approach
- Determine the total budget.
- Choose the property based on the total budget.
- Commute time.
- Supporting facilities.

## 2. Before Buying
### 2.1. Down Payment
- Parents.
- Help relatives and friends manage money.
- Borrow from a bank.
    - The original note preferred a mortgage over full payment.
    - Borrow as much as possible within one's capacity.
    - Prefer a longer loan term.
    - Choose equal principal-and-interest repayment.
    - Use a housing-provident-fund loan.

- Offer the landlord/seller the desired price.

#### 3.1.5. Agent
- Negotiate the agent fee near the point of closing rather than at the very beginning.
- Watch for sales tactics.
    - Is this home really suitable to buy?
    - Are there really that many competing buyers?
    - "Getting a bargain" framing.

#### 3.1.6. Timing
The historical note suggested buying before Spring Festival could be advantageous. This is a historical judgment, not a stable rule.

## 6. Real-Estate Knowledge
### 6.1. Logic of Property Tax
The original note reasons from public infrastructure and land/property appreciation: if a nearby metro raises property values while construction is funded publicly, some of the benefit may be captured through taxation.

It then raises the burden on middle-class mortgage borrowers and discusses a historical argument about lowering debt-service costs while collecting property tax. This section is preserved as the original line of thought, not as a statement of current policy.
