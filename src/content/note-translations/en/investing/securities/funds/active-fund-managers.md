## 1. Recommended Active Fund Managers
- If a fund manager runs several funds, their holdings are often similar; the note argues they should not be spread across too many distinct portfolios because the manager's attention is limited.
- The note preferred funds that were not too large because very large portfolios can be harder to operate. It recorded roughly 8 billion yuan for large-cap funds and 4 billion yuan for small-cap funds as already relatively large historical thresholds.
- Prefer managers with mixed-industry mandates. Without an industry restriction, the note believed managers could adjust allocations to control drawdown.

## 2. E Fund
- Zhang Kun
    - Graduated from Tsinghua University in Biomedical Engineering.
    - Fund
        - ![](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/TDoct/images/master/1604159673_20201031234558273_5392.png)
    - Characteristics
        - Bottom-up.
        - Low turnover.
            - How did the note describe his candidate-pool criteria?
                - 1. Average ROIC over the past 5-10 years no lower than 10%, without large fluctuations.
                - 2. Exclude companies with excessive leverage; prefer businesses that grow organically and accumulate cash flow through operations rather than heavily indebted asset-heavy models.
                - 3. Strong industry position and a deep moat/barrier.
        - High individual-stock concentration; top-10 holdings around 70% in the historical snapshot.
        - Emphasizes margin of safety and drawdown control.
        - Circle of competence
            - Missing opportunities outside one's competence.
            - Failing to do well within one's competence.
            - Entering an unfamiliar area and making mistakes.
