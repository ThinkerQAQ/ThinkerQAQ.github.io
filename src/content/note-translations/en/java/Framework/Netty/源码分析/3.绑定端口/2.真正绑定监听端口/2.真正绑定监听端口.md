After channel creation/initialization/registration, the bind operation is executed on the channel's event-loop context. The concrete transport binds the underlying socket/local address and the channel can transition to active/listening state.

The returned `ChannelFuture` completes asynchronously with success/failure. Application startup should observe that completion rather than assuming a bind call succeeded immediately.

Native/JDK calls, backlog handling, and pipeline event details are version/transport-specific.
